Overview

Introduction

During the 2025-2026 school year, I had the opportunity to participate in the Howard County Public School System's Gifted & Talented Research Program. The program requires participants to complete their own original research throughout the school year, completing steps such as secondary research, primary research, and a comprehensive research board and paper using the information and skills they attained throughout the year.

Background

Cancer maintains a relatively high mortality rate partially due to the difficulty in identifying and treating cancerous cells early, which is often reliant on human judgement, leading to potential errors and oversight. Because the more conventional methods of diagnosis, such as manually looking through radiographic images for signs of cancer, are limited by human error and inability to process complex data, they can be very ineffective for the early detection of cancer. Likewise, the treatment of such cancer is also impacted. The question then becomes: how can the limitations of these methods be addressed to improve both the accuracy of early detection and the effectiveness of treatment?
